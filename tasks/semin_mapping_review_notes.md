Verdicts assess whether the full entry belongs under its current surah/ayah association. A MISMATCH in Turkish/English generally means the translation follows Arabic commentary whose current association is too narrow; it is not a finding that the translation is mistranslated. Supporting Quran quotations, examples, pronoun antecedents and possible grammatical governors are not automatically additional primary associations. Direct independent lexical, reading or grammatical exposition is substantive commentary.

The proposed associations are evidence for a decision queue, not automatically authorized production changes. Source edition/PDF and a second reviewer should adjudicate proposed expansions and mixed entries.
